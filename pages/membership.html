California Highway Patrol

Information

The California Highway Patrol provides statewide highway enforcement, traffic enforcement, investigations, and specialized law enforcement services.

Points

  • •Must be in the LEAP Discord
  • •Must meet LEAP activity requirements
  • •Must maintain good standing in LEAP

Requirements

  • •Must be a member of the LEAP Discord
  • •Must follow all LEAP Regulations
  • •Must remain active
  • •CHP is a Handpicked Agency
